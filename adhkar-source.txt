This summary of the authentic morning and evening Adhkar has been compiled from the works
and publications of Shaykh Abdul Aziz At-Tarefe. It has been concisely written in a
straightforward manner to inspire Muslims of all levels wishing to reconnect back to Allah .
One way they can establish this is by performing the Daily Adhkar. By doing so, one will always
be in a continual state of consciousness of Allah whilst protecting themselves from all types of
evil and harm, which can impede their success in this world and the Hereafter.
